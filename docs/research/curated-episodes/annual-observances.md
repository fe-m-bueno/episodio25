# Annual observances: curated episode research

Research date: 2026-08-20  
Scope: `christmas`, `halloween`, `valentines-day`, `thanksgiving`, `new-years-eve`, `easter`, `mothers-day`, `fathers-day`

## Executive summary

This report identifies 64 viable episode candidates across the eight annual-observance themes. Every theme has at least seven candidates, and every pool contains at least five distinct series, so the later five-episode editorial selection can satisfy the repository's diversity rules without depending on a single franchise. The pool includes 22 animation candidates and two explicitly classified anime candidates.

Most rows are high confidence because an official network, studio, or distributor page establishes both episode identity and an observance-driven plot. Six rows are medium confidence: their thematic fit is well supported, but regional storefronts, segment packaging, or external metadata disagree about season/episode numbering. Those conflicts are recorded rather than reconciled by inference. `K-ON!` is the only accepted series whose public TMDB series mapping could not be established confidently during this pass; it is marked `mapping-pending`.

Apple TV is the most common first-party distributor source in the tables. Its episode labels and synopses are useful, but dates shown by some regional storefronts can reflect local availability rather than original broadcast. Where that problem was apparent, the original air date is either cross-checked against a public TMDB episode record or explicitly qualified.

## Methodology

- Applied the repository candidate schema to every row: canonical slug, series and format, exact episode identity, original air date where verifiable, TMDB series mapping, observance-specific rationale, evidence, confidence, and caveats.
- Used official network/studio pages when available (ABC, Bluey, Channel 4, Netflix, Paramount+, PBS Kids), followed by official distributor episode pages (principally Apple TV). Secondary episode guides were used only to locate or cross-check candidates, never as the sole reason for accepting one.
- Required the observance to drive the setting, central event, or a material plot decision. A matching title or seasonal air date by itself was not enough.
- Treated TMDB IDs as identity/mapping coordinates, not thematic evidence. The numeric ID in each row links to a public TMDB series URL.
- Preserved conflicts among broadcast order, paired-segment order, regional storefront order, and TMDB order. Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; `unverified` means the available first-party page did not establish a reliable original date.

## Source and mapping notes

1. **First-party hierarchy.** Official series/network pages were preferred for plot: [Bluey](https://www.bluey.tv/watch/), [Netflix Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/), [Channel 4](https://www.channel4.com/press/news/peep-show), [Paramount+](https://www.paramountplus.com/), and [PBS Kids](https://pbskids.org/videos/cyberchase?source=googlehome). Apple TV was treated as an official distributor episode guide, not as an infallible original-broadcast database.
2. **Apple dates.** Several regional Apple pages display local storefront dates, generic dates, or a date one day away because of timezone handling. The affected rows explicitly say so. Their episode titles, order labels, and synopses remain useful first-party distributor evidence.
3. **Segmented animation.** *Hey Arnold!*, *Rugrats*, *Teen Titans Go!*, *Peppa Pig*, and sometimes *Bluey* are packaged differently across broadcast, paired-segment, and streaming catalogs. Rows with a demonstrated conflict are `medium` or carry an episode-mapping warning even when the observance fit is strong.
4. **TMDB policy.** A linked TMDB series ID means the series identity was resolved from a public URL. It does not imply that TMDB's episode order agrees with the cited distributor. `mapping-pending` means the series or episode coordinate should not be guessed during manifest work.
5. **Cross-theme reuse.** *Brooklyn Nine-Nine* “Valloweaster” genuinely supports Easter as well as Halloween and Valentine's Day, but the rationale is multi-observance. It is retained as a medium-confidence Easter reserve, not as a preferred first-load choice.
6. **Selection recommendation.** Prefer high-confidence rows with unconflicted episode identities for the first five selections. Keep medium-confidence rows as reserves until the manifest hydrator's canonical episode-order policy is decided.
