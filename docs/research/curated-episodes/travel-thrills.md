# Travel, outings, thrills, and contests episode research

Status: editorial research complete; TMDB season-response hydration still required for rows carrying numbering caveats.

Research date: 2026-08-20.

## Executive summary

This slice identifies 56 viable episode candidates across the seven assigned themes: eight candidates per theme. The set is deliberately broader than the five-episode release minimum and has enough series diversity to survive editorial cuts without violating the two-series rule. Animation appears in every theme where it is editorially strong, and anime appears in `camping-trip`, `ghost-haunted-house`, and `tournament-competition`.

The strongest ready-to-shortlist groups are `road-trip`, `festival-fair`, and `ghost-haunted-house`. `beach-day` and `vacation-trip` have several strong candidates but also contain distributor bundle numbering that must be reconciled against the TMDB season response before compound keys are committed. `camping-trip` has seven high-confidence candidates without relying on the particularly unstable SpongeBob segment numbering. `tournament-competition` is intentionally interpreted according to the approved alias boundary—tournaments, contests, championships, and materially competitive events all qualify—but ordinary games without meaningful competitive stakes were excluded.

TMDB IDs below are identity pointers only. They do not prove thematic relevance or episode numbering. `mapping-pending` means no sufficiently clear public TMDB series URL was found during this pass; it is preferable to a guessed ID.

## Methodology

1. Started from the approved theme labels, aliases, and materiality rule in the canonical manifest.
2. Located candidates using broad searches, then required plot-level confirmation from an official network/studio guide or an authorized distributor episode page. Apple TV episode pages are treated as first-party distributor metadata, not as proof that their regional release date or bundle numbering matches TMDB.
3. Checked exact series title, displayed season/episode identity, title, plot relevance, and date where the source exposed a plausible original date.
4. Recorded regional, segment, translation, and multipart conflicts instead of normalizing them by intuition.
5. Added a TMDB series ID only when a public series URL was known or discoverable. Episode keys still require a later lookup against that exact TMDB season response.
6. Classified format editorially: `anime` only for explicitly identified Japanese anime; other drawn/CG series are `animation`; all others are `live-action`.

Confidence means thematic and identity confidence at this research stage:

- `high`: the cited plot description makes the theme material and the displayed episode identity is coherent.
- `medium`: the theme is material, but numbering, bundling, date, or identity needs explicit reconciliation before hydration.
- `reject`: evidence does not cross the theme boundary.

## Source notes

- Official network/studio pages used here include Bluey/BBC Studios, Pokémon, Paramount+/CBS/Nickelodeon, Netflix, and Crunchyroll. Authorized Apple TV episode pages supplied distributor metadata for series whose network guides were unavailable or less stable.
- Apple TV metadata is region-sensitive. A regional page may show a local purchase, dub, or package date rather than the original broadcast date; implausible dates were omitted and conflicts were documented.
- Apple also packages short-form animation in multiple ways: standalone segments, two-segment broadcasts, and themed compilations can all expose different episode numbers. Every such row is marked `medium` or held out.
- TMDB links identify the series only. The next curation pass must request the exact TMDB season and select the returned episode number; this report intentionally does not infer a compound key from another service’s numbering.
- Where a source gives only a year, the report does not silently manufacture a precise date from secondary memory.
