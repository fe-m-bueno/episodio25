# Theme discovery strategy

## Question

How can the app produce useful thematic results when TMDB does not classify every individual episode perfectly?

## Resolution

Use a hybrid approach. Local theme configuration provides labels, descriptions, keywords, and curated seeds. TMDB supplies current content details and keyword-based discovery. Episode matching combines selected episode records with title and overview relevance.
