# Curated episode catalog audit

Status: superseded before completion by the generated thematic episode index design. Retained as an acceptance-checklist reference.

## Acceptance gates

An episode-theme association is accepted only when all applicable gates pass:

- [ ] The canonical theme slug exists.
- [ ] The theme is material to the plot, setting, or central event.
- [ ] Plot evidence comes from a high-trust source and is cited.
- [ ] Series title, episode title, season number, and episode number are verified.
- [ ] Any alternate numbering or multipart ambiguity is documented.
- [ ] The TMDB series ID and compound episode coordinates resolve consistently.
- [ ] The rationale describes this specific episode-theme association.
- [ ] The editorial format is supported rather than inferred from language alone.
- [ ] The same compound episode identity is not duplicated elsewhere.
- [ ] The candidate is not accepted solely because of its title or broadcast date.
