# Curated episode catalog research

## Question

Which individual TV and anime episodes can be supported by strong evidence as material matches for each of the 30 canonical themes, while satisfying the approved identity, diversity, and editorial quality rules?

## Resolution

Stopped by user decision after three completed research slices demonstrated that complete manual curation would remain too small and labor-intensive. The [annual observances](../../../../docs/research/curated-episodes/annual-observances.md), [seasons and school](../../../../docs/research/curated-episodes/seasons-school.md), and [travel, outings, thrills, and contests](../../../../docs/research/curated-episodes/travel-thrills.md) reports are retained as classifier benchmark and audit material. Complete manual episode-catalog curation is superseded by the generated thematic episode index.
