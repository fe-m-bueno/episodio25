# Canonical theme manifest and quality bar

## Question

What canonical slug, English label, editorial group, and minimum quality criteria should each of the 30 approved themes contain, before TMDB keyword IDs and curated item references are populated?

## Resolution

Approved the canonical 30-theme vocabulary and release quality bar in [Canonical theme manifest](../../../../docs/plans/2026-08-20-theme-manifest-proposal.md). Every theme must launch with at least eight verified items, including five curated episodes and two movies, with diversity, rationale, drift checks, and a 180-day review cycle.
