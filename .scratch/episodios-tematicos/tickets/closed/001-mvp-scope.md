# MVP destination and client experience

## Question

What should the first product increment deliver?

## Resolution

Build an English-language, client-focused SvelteKit app deployed to Vercel. It includes theme browsing, free search, content details, filters, sorting, and regional provider availability. A small server-side SvelteKit proxy is allowed and required to protect TMDB credentials.
