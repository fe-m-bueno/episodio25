# Curated episode research

Status: stopped after three completed slices; retained as classifier benchmark and audit material.

## Objective

Produce an evidence-backed shortlist of individual TV and anime episodes for all 30 canonical themes. The release catalog requires at least five verified episodes per theme; research should preserve extra candidates where the evidence is strong enough to support later editorial selection.

## Candidate schema

Each candidate records:

- canonical theme slug;
- series title and editorial format (`live-action`, `animation`, or `anime`);
- exact season number, episode number, and episode title;
- original air date when verifiable;
- TMDB series ID or an explicit `mapping-pending` marker;
- a theme-specific rationale explaining why the theme is material to the episode;
- evidence links and what each source establishes;
- confidence (`high`, `medium`, or `reject`);
- numbering, translation, multipart, or identity caveats.

## Evidence standard

1. Prefer the official network, studio, distributor, or series episode guide for plot evidence.
2. Use first-party database records or stable episode guides to verify identity and numbering.
3. A title match alone is insufficient. The synopsis or another reliable description must show that the theme materially affects the plot, setting, or central event.
4. When numbering differs between broadcast, streaming, DVD, or regional releases, record the conflict instead of guessing.
5. Season `0` specials are allowed only when the chosen identity resolves consistently.
6. Reject listicle-only claims, uncited fan recollections, passing decorations, and episodes where the theme is merely inferred from an air date.
7. TMDB numeric IDs are drift guards and hydration coordinates, not evidence that an episode matches a theme.

## Selection rules

- Target at least seven researched candidates per theme so five can survive final review.
- The final first-load set must include episodes from at least two series.
- No more than two of the first eight items may come from the same series or franchise.
- Preserve strong anime and animation candidates explicitly; do not infer anime solely from language or country.
- One episode may support multiple themes only when each association has separate evidence and rationale.

## Research slices

- `annual-observances.md`: Christmas through Father's Day.
- `personal-relationships.md`: personal celebrations and relationships or gatherings.
- `seasons-school.md`: seasons, school, and school events.
- `travel-thrills.md`: travel, outings, supernatural themes, and competitions.

The personal and relationship slice was not completed. The finished annual-observance, seasons-and-school, and travel-and-thrills reports remain reference material for the generated-index classifier; they are not a complete manually curated catalog.
