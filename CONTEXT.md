# Domain glossary

## Theme

An editorial category describing a recurring situation, event, season, relationship, or setting represented by media content. Examples include Halloween, graduation, camping, and first date.

## Thematic item

A catalog entry associated with a theme. A thematic item is either a movie or a specific episode.

## Movie

A feature-length media item represented by its TMDB movie identity.

## Episode

A single installment of a television series or anime, identified by its series, season, and episode number.

## Provider availability

The set of ways a thematic item can be watched in a country, such as subscription streaming, free streaming, rental, or purchase.

## Country context

The two-letter ISO country code used to interpret provider availability for a request.

## Curated seed

A deliberately selected movie, series, or episode associated with a theme to ensure a useful starting catalog.

## Theme match

The reason an item appears for a theme. A match can be curated or inferred from TMDB keywords and episode text.
